Design Tools

Self-built generative design toys. Each tool is one self-contained HTML file under tools/ — no build step, no dependencies.

  • Kinetic Type Text warped by sine-wave oscillators — 3 variants: grid warp, stripes × difference, sliced liquid type.
  • Flow-Field Ink Particles drifting through a vector field, leaving ink trails — 3 fields: silk, vortex, turbulence. Click to wipe.
  • Halftone Imager Drop any image; it gets rasterized into dots, lines or ASCII by brightness. Right-click to save.
  • Reaction-Diffusion Gray-Scott chemistry growing coral, cells and mazes. Drag to paint chemical; feed/kill sliders steer the regime.
  • Physics Type Letters as verlet bodies you can grab and throw — springs home, hanging chain, or a gravity pit.
  • Dot Bridge Draw on a dot grid; strokes become circles joined by smooth necks. Symmetry, grow, and SVG export for Figma.
  • Truchet Tiler Paint a grid of two-way tiles into mazes, worms and blobs — arcs, two-tone fill or diagonals, with symmetry.
